# Changelog

All notable changes to the dPLaaX protocol specification and namespace documents
are documented here. The format is based on
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project
adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

While the version is `0.x`, a rule may still be deleted, or the meaning of its
statement changed, between minor releases (VERSIONING.md). A namespace
document under `https://dplaax.dev/` is frozen once shipped: its meaning never
changes, and a semantic change takes a new path. An editorial fix is allowed,
except in a JSON-LD context, which is frozen to the byte (`site/README.md`;
the Namespace change class in GOVERNANCE.md). A branch context snapshot is
frozen from its merge into `develop` (`contexts/README.md`).

## [0.2.0-alpha.1] - 2026-11-11

A pre-release on the way to 0.2. The conditions for the 0.2 tag
(VERSIONING.md) are not yet met: 118 of the 257 rules have a conformance
vector.

### Added

- Branch contexts: the terms of a feature on a base version of the
  protocol context, each snapshot frozen at
  `https://dplaax.dev/vc/branches/<base>/<feature>/<stamp>` (UTC
  `YYYYMMDDHHmm`), only adding terms within a branch, and folded into the
  next base version once confirmed (`contexts/README.md`). The first is
  `https://dplaax.dev/vc/branches/v1/keybinding/202611101543`, the key-binding
  terms; sha256 `cc07379a021cad29b2e8f5c137a2d6c185e283cc523c7d59ef360b58f6594916`, pinned in
  `contexts/branches/SHA256SUMS`. `https://dplaax.dev/vc/v1` is unchanged.
  `tools/lint.py` checks the branch contexts: their paths and stamps, their
  pins, add-only within a branch, one definition per term name on a base,
  and, against the base ref CI passes, that no shipped snapshot changed.
- `keybinding`: rules for key bindings, an issuer's signed statement that a
  key is a subject's for one relying party, and their revocation lists:
  `keybinding.shape`, `.context`, `.issuer`, `.validity`, `.audience`, `.status.entry`,
  `.status-list.shape`, `.status-list.id`, `.status-list.period`, `.status-list.content-hash`,
  `.status-list.monotonic` (reasons `status-list-rewound` and
  `status-list-revocation-cleared`) and `.status-list.fresh`. Schemas
  `key-binding-credential.json` and `status-list-credential.json`; vectors
  `keybinding-001`..`054`, generated by `tools/gen_keybinding_vectors.py`.
  `tools/validate_vectors.py` also validates the lists a key-binding vector
  carries beside its credential.
- `formal/tamarin/keybinding.spthy`: a Tamarin model of key bindings and
  their revocation lists (whose binding a relying party takes, for whom,
  and whether a revocation can be undone), with nine negative controls.
  Non-normative.
- `formal/`: a Tamarin model of provenance-chain authentication
  (`formal/tamarin/chain.spthy`) with its contract, the lemma-to-rule table
  (`formal/README.md`), a negative control (`-D=SkipLinkCheck`) and CI
  (`.github/workflows/formal.yml`). Non-normative; the rules are unchanged.
- `formal/tamarin/delegation.spthy`: a Tamarin model of delegation from an
  Owner to its Pipelines and Processes (who can delegate, over what, and
  what a delegation is used for), with six negative controls. Non-normative;
  the rules are unchanged.
- `delegation.use.subject-bound`: a consumer treats a delegation as authority
  only for the identity whose DID is byte-identical to its
  `credentialSubject.id`, not for another identity under the same Owner nor
  for a Process under a delegated Pipeline. Vectors `delegation-006`
  (accepted), `delegation-007` and `delegation-008` (rejected).
- `did`: the did:dplaax DID method. `did.dplaax.syntax` fixes its syntax
  (`did:dplaax:<registry>:<accountType>:<accountId>` and resource-path
  segments, each `[A-Za-z0-9._-]+` and not dots only, the registry at most
  253 octets), and `did.dplaax.hierarchy` reads an Owner, a Pipeline
  (`pipeline:<id>`) or a Process (`pipeline:<id>:process:<id>`) from it.
  Vectors `did-001`..`031`, generated by `tools/gen_did_vectors.py`.
  `tools/lint.py` requires a vector for every `did` rule, as for the seven
  wire-core families.
- `effect-gated`, a conformance class beside `core` and `audit-reachable`:
  it binds an exact accepted evidence view to an external-effect state
  machine (`rules/external-effect.yaml`). Like `audit-reachable`, it is
  opt-in. `GLOSSARY.md` defines it, and `tools/rule.schema.json` and
  `tools/lint.py` accept it.
- Vectors `evidence-view-001` and `evidence-view-002` for
  `evidence.manifest.identity`. CI checks them with
  `tools/verify_evidence_vectors.py`, a checker that shares no code with
  provin-line/oss.
- `GOVERNANCE.md`: roles, change classes and their procedures, promotion
  from `draft` to `stable`, the decision method, succession and forks, and
  IPR.
- A branch guard (`.github/workflows/branch-guard.yml`): a pull request into
  `main` from a fork, or from a branch other than this repository's
  `develop`, `release/*` and `hotfix/*`, fails its `branch-guard` check.
  Other pull requests target `develop`, as `CONTRIBUTING.md` says.

### Changed

- `auth.grant.exact-method`: a signed absolute method id is required of the
  A3.1 contracts (`OWNER_AUTHENTICATION_LOGIN@1`,
  `OWNER_ASSERTION_CONTROL_LOGIN@1`). A `LEGACY_DID_LOGIN@1` request may omit
  it only when the resolved document has exactly one controller-matched
  method; first-match, try-all and array-order dependence stay rejected under
  every contract. Vectors `auth-grant-legacy-no-method-001` (issued) and
  `auth-grant-legacy-no-method-ambiguous-001` (refused).
- `auth.grant.kid-match`: each method reference a request carries (the JWS
  protected kid, the signed-payload method id) must be the resolver-selected
  id, and an A3.1 contract request carries both. A legacy request naming
  neither has nothing to match.
- `vectors/README.md`, section "Fixture proofs": the proofs in the vectors
  are fixtures, with a placeholder `proofValue` and no proof-local
  `@context`. A driver re-signs each credential by eddsa-jcs-2022, adding
  only those two members, except where an expected value digests a
  credential with its proof (the `identity.*` ids, the `commitment.*` source
  roots, and documents carried as bytes). No vector changed.
- Vectors `effect-001` and `effect-002`: their input carries well-formed
  did:dplaax DIDs (`did.dplaax.syntax`) in place of two-segment strings.
  Their expected values are unchanged. Of the vectors in 0.1.0, they are the
  only ones whose bytes changed.
- 33 rules move from `core` to `effect-gated`: the 28 rules of
  `rules/external-effect.yaml` (`effect.*`), and
  `claims.profile.external-effect`, `claims.effect.contracts`,
  `claims.effect.legacy-receipt`, `claims.effect.scope-mapping` and
  `audit.release.synthesis`. Their statements are unchanged.
- `https://dplaax.dev/` is served from S3 and CloudFront (`infra/deploy.md`)
  in place of GitHub Pages, so the extensionless identifiers are served
  directly with their media types: `/vc/v1` as `application/ld+json`, and
  `/oauth/grant-type/did` as `text/html` with 200, where Pages redirected it
  with a 301 to `/oauth/grant-type/did/`. `/vc/v1`, the schemas already
  served, the grant-type document and the apex pages keep their bytes.

### Removed

- From `https://dplaax.dev/`, the paths GitHub Pages served and the S3 and
  CloudFront deploy does not: `/README.md`, `/CNAME` and `/.nojekyll` (files
  under `site/`), `/oauth/grant-type/did/` with its trailing slash, and
  `/oauth/grant-type/did/index.html`. Each now returns 404. The identifier
  `/oauth/grant-type/did` is served directly (see Changed).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-17

Initial public release of the specification draft.

### Added

- The `did:dplaax` DID method and registry model.
- The DID grant-type namespace document for
  `https://dplaax.dev/oauth/grant-type/did`, published at the wire-identifier
  path (the identifier is compared byte-for-byte).
- Conformance and protocol drafts for the provenance data plane, the audit /
  reconciliation model, and the cross-organization export seam.
- The first auth conformance vectors: kid-match, DID id-equality, and
  resolution failure-mapping.
- Namespace documents and JSON Schemas served at their production URIs under
  `https://dplaax.dev/`.
